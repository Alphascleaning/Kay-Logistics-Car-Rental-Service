About Kay Logistics Car Rental Service (KLCRS)

Kay Logistics Car Rental Service (KLCRS) is a trusted vehicle rental provider based in Wellington, Freetown, Sierra Leone. We specialize in offering reliable jeeps and SUVs for both inland and upland travel routes.

Our mission is simple: provide safe, well‑maintained vehicles with transparent pricing and professional service. Whether you're traveling within Freetown or heading to the provinces, KLCRS ensures a smooth and secure journey.

Our Policies

  • GPS Tracking: All vehicles are equipped with trackers for safety and route monitoring.
  • Fuel Policy: Vehicles come with reserve fuel and must be returned with the same quantity.
  • Driver Responsibility: If you provide your own driver, you are responsible for any damages.
  • Unauthorized Travel: Travel outside the agreed rental area incurs additional fees.

Our Commitment

KLCRS is committed to providing dependable transportation solutions for individuals, businesses, and travelers. With clear communication, fair pricing, and strict safety standards, we aim to be Sierra Leone’s most reliable car rental service.
